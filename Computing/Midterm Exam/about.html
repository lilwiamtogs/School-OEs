About the Haus

Made for the people who stay a little longer.

Koffee Haus is a coffee-and-culture blog inspired by Antipolo, bright blue skies, and the comfort of a familiar table.

Our story

A small cafe idea with a wide orbit.

The round planet in our logo says everything: coffee brings different people into the same orbit. Koffee Haus celebrates that feeling through drink stories, simple cafe guides, and thoughtful notes about slowing down.

The "Antipolo 2008" stamp grounds the brand in a place and a memory. Our visual identity carries that spirit forward with energetic cobalt blue, generous white space, and playful circular details.
