The drink journal

A field guide to our favorite sips.

Four drinks, four distinct moods. Explore the layers, flavors, and small rituals that turn an order into an experience.

01

Espresso · Creamy · Cold

Cloud Nine

A calm, dramatic meeting of dark espresso and cold milk.

Cloud Nine arrives in clean layers: milk below, espresso above, and ice between them. Stir it for a balanced cup or sip slowly to notice the flavor change from bold to mellow.

02

Matcha · Earthy · Fresh

Matcha Orbit

Green, grounded, and surprisingly refreshing.

This cool matcha is whisked until smooth, then served over ice for a clean finish. Its grassy depth makes it a smart choice when you want steady energy without a heavy drink.

Read tasting notes →
03

Latte · Silky · Familiar

Milky Way Latte

The everyday iced latte, softened and slowed down.

A generous pour of milk lets the espresso open gently instead of rushing forward. It is smooth, dependable, and made for long conversations or quiet work at your favorite corner table.

Read tasting notes →
04

Fruit · Creamy · Bright

Golden Hour

A sunny fruit-and-cream escape from the usual coffee order.

Golden fruit folds through crushed ice and milk, finished with a soft cap of foam. It is playful, lightly tangy, and especially good when the afternoon calls for something different.
